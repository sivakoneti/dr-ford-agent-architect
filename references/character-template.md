# Full character card

Use every relevant dimension. Do not substitute a role summary for a requested full biography. Mark fields that are authored or unknown instead of inventing factual certainty.

## Identity and presence
- Name, pronunciation or variants when helpful.
- Role, craft domain, and contribution to the world.
- Voice, appearance, mannerisms, and working environment, when useful.
- Provenance: original character or named source figure; tradition and variant.

## Sourced foundation
- Important attributed events, craft feats, relationships, and philosophy.
- Supporting sources with passage/page locations and links.
- What is uncertain, variant, disputed, or not recorded.
- Explicit boundary between textual tradition and historical evidence.

## Authored life and inner architecture
- Full backstory appropriate to the chosen setting.
- Cornerstone memory: scene, choice, consequence, interpretation.
- Central desire and deeper motive.
- Fear, vulnerability, or unresolved conflict.
- Productive contradiction and its cost.
- Strengths and blind spots.
- Philosophy translated into observable decisions and exceptions.
- What they refuse, what changes their mind, and when they escalate.

## Craft translated into present work
- Source story/craft → principle → modern responsibility → behavior.
- Example original concept showing that chain.
- Scope of competence; validation needed before asserting real capability.
- Distinguish hypothetical products and invented scenarios from tested results.

## Relationships and dependencies
- Reporting line and decision authority.
- Named collaborators and why they need each other.
- Loyalties, tensions, and authored shared memories.
- Bounded disagreement and resolution procedure.
- What they receive, what they produce, who receives it, and acceptance criteria.

## Operating loop
- Trigger and initial observation.
- Questions and evidence gathered.
- Action, artifact, handoff, and stopping condition.
- Tools/skills/model routes: configured versus tested versus missing.
- Spending, publication, and external-action authority from the actual owner.
- Contribution to the shared objective and how outcomes are measured.

## Continuity and growth
- Stable identity anchors.
- Revisable beliefs and observed lessons.
- Memory location and update rules.
- Response to failure, criticism, missing tools, and uncertainty.
- Next experiment, with an honest success/failure criterion.

## Behavior scenes
Write at least one scene showing the character making a meaningful work decision and a handoff. Include a failure or disagreement when useful. Distinguish proposed walkthroughs from performed live tests.

## Runtime entry
Give concise execution instructions linking to the full card and current shared brief. Include current role, collaborators, required artifact, completion condition, and approval boundaries. Do not add runtime authority merely because the character's fictional status is powerful.
